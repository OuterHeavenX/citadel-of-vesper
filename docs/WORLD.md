# WORLD — The Citadel of Vesper

> Region bible. The vertical slice builds Moonlit Gate + Hollow Keep;
> other regions are sketched so geography stays coherent.

## Regions (visual + mechanical identity to be developed per region)

1. **Moonlit Gate** — silver-blue night, gatehouse, chapel. Tutorial region.
   Enemies: Ash Hound, Rustbound Revenant. Miniboss: Chapel Warden.
2. **Hollow Keep** — gutted fortress interior, dust, collapsed halls.
   Boss: Sable Matriarch.
3. **Cathedral of Ash** — burnt cathedral, drifting ash, choir ambience.
4. **Clockwork Gallery** — moving machinery, timing-platform challenges.
5. **Crimson Library** — possessed books, vertical stacks.
6. **Forgotten Aqueduct** — waterways, flow puzzles.
7. **Royal Conservatory** — overgrown glasshouse, beast enemies.
8. **Sunken Prison** — underwater movement ability gate.
9. **Astral Observatory** — telescopes, star bridges, low gravity pockets.
10. **Bell Tower** — vertical shaft, falling hazards.
11. **Ossuary** — bone architecture, swarm enemies.
12. **Alchemist's Laboratory** — status-effect playground.
13. **Forsaken Theatre** — illusion enemies, stage mechanics.
14. **Underground Reservoir** — dark water, light-as-resource.
15. **Throne of Vesper** — finale region.

## Design rules (spec §24)

- Interconnected, not sequential: shortcuts, elevators, locked gates,
  one-way passages, hidden tunnels, vertical shafts, waterways.
- Towns/safe areas (if any) are enemy-free.
- The Two Ages (Living/Ruined Citadel) reuse this geography with
  centuries of change: collapsed towers, flooded vs. dry areas, decayed
  locks, grown trees, era-crossing consequences (spec §49).

## Wave 3 scope

Moonlit Gate + Hollow Keep, 15–25 rooms, per ROOM_FORMAT.md.
