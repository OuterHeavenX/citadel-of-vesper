# STORY — The Citadel of Vesper

> Original dark gothic story. Tone: mysterious, melancholy, supernatural,
> tragic, philosophical. Told through environment, item descriptions, NPCs,
> journals, bosses, architecture, hidden lore — never exposition dumps.

## Premise (Wave 0 canon — do not contradict)

- During a celestial event (the **Vesper Conjunction**), an enormous
  fortress-city **appears** where no fortress stood: the Citadel of Vesper.
- **Lucien Vale**, disgraced member of the ancient **Order of the Pale
  Hour**, comes because his bloodline is mysteriously connected to the
  Citadel — and the Order has disowned anyone who asks why.
- The Citadel exists **simultaneously across different periods of history**.
  The player discovers this slowly; the late-game revelation is the
  **Two Ages of Vesper**: the Living Citadel and the Ruined Citadel, and
  the power to move between them (spec §49).

## Story delivery rules

- NPCs relocate as the story advances; some quests have different outcomes.
- Item descriptions and bestiary lore carry history, not the dialogue box.
- Bosses are story: each one's lore explains a piece of the Citadel.
- Wave 4+ expands: journals, the scholar NPC, the rival explorer.

## Reserved mysteries (do not resolve early)

- Why the Order disgraced Lucien.
- What the Vesper Conjunction actually was.
- Who built the Citadel — and when "when" is.
