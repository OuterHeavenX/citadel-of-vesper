# ROADMAP — The Citadel of Vesper

> Milestones after Wave 0. Quality before quantity (master prompt §70).

## v0.1.0 ALPHA — Vertical slice (Waves 1–7)

Moonlit Gate → Hollow Keep, 15–25 rooms. Title, New Game/Continue,
movement, combat, 8 enemies, equipment, inventory, leveling, map, save
system + save room, hidden rooms, miniboss (Chapel Warden), major boss
(Sable Matriarch), traversal ability (Moonrise Leap), teleport chamber,
keyboard + controller + touch, music + SFX, effects, Pages deployment.
Definition of done: master prompt §70 checklist.

## v0.2.0 — The Citadel opens

Cathedral of Ash + Clockwork Gallery. Spells (Cinder Wave, Mending
Pulse). Merchant + scholar NPCs. More weapons/armor. Bestiary UI.

## v0.3.0 — Depths

Crimson Library, Forgotten Aqueduct, Sunken Prison (underwater movement).
More bosses, side quests.

## v0.4.0 — Heights

Astral Observatory, Bell Tower, Royal Conservatory. Air dash traversal.

## v0.5.0 — The Two Ages of Vesper

Living Citadel / Ruined Citadel era travel — the major late-game
revelation (spec §49). Ossuary, Alchemist's Laboratory, Forsaken
Theatre, Underground Reservoir.

## v1.0 — Throne of Vesper

Finale region, true ending, completionist content (25–40h target).

## Non-goals

- No paid assets. No Godot branding. No game jams.
- Towns/safe spaces stay enemy-free.
